# Training Data and Checkpoint Redistribution Audit

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30. This is a release-control record, not legal advice.

**Checkpoint publication status: HOLD.** The available terms include non-commercial
restrictions and do not clearly authorize redistribution of trained model parameters across
every active source. This repository therefore publishes software, configurations, aggregate
evidence, report, and attributed demonstration media, but no weights.

| Source | Role | Public terms reviewed | Release decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| P-DESTRE | cross-camera evaluation and derived demo media | [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.02782); project-held CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 notice | dataset not redistributed; derived media attributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; weights held |
| MOT17 / MOT20 | detector and tracker training | [MOTChallenge](https://motchallenge.net/data/MOT17/) | no blanket weight-redistribution permission identified; hold |
| SOMPT22 | detector and tracker training | [project terms](https://sompt22.github.io/) | non-commercial research restriction; hold |
| CrowdHuman | additional detector source | [download terms](https://www.crowdhuman.org/download.html) | image redistribution prohibited and weight status unclear; hold |

No dataset, source video, annotation archive, checkpoint, or person-level benchmark record is
committed. Publication requires written clarification for every source plus artifact-equivalence
and checksum verification.
